# Governance

OpenPreflight begins with one maintainer. The maintainer triages issues, reviews
changes, protects releases, coordinates security response, keeps the roadmap,
and appoints additional maintainers based on sustained trustworthy contribution.
Decisions seek consensus; the maintainer records nontrivial outcomes in ADRs and
has final release authority. Security responders may embargo details and make an
emergency patch decision.

Commit access requires consistent review quality, DCO compliance, secure
handling of reports, and two-factor authentication. Contributors must not approve
their own material trust-boundary changes when another qualified reviewer is
available. Conflicts are documented, parties recuse when necessary, and an
independent contributor is invited to mediate. Maintainers inactive for six
months are contacted privately; after another month they may be moved to emeritus
status. No commercial sponsor receives automatic authority.
