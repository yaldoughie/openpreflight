# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual and romantic identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful feedback, accepting
responsibility, focusing on community needs, and welcoming different viewpoints.
Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized conduct, trolling, insults, public or
private harassment, publishing private information, or other conduct reasonably
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers clarify and enforce standards and may edit, reject, or remove
contributions or temporarily/permanently ban participants. Report incidents
privately using the `REPLACE_BEFORE_RELEASE_SECURITY_CONTACT` listed in the
release checklist. Do not use a public issue. Until that real monitored contact
is configured, this repository must not be publicly released.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant impact guidelines: correction for
minor inappropriate behavior, warning for repeated incidents, temporary ban for
serious or sustained violations, and permanent ban for patterns of abuse or
harassment. Reports are handled confidentially and conflicts are recused.
